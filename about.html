Our Story

A Legacy of Engineering Excellence

Founded in 2060 BS (Nepali Calendar) in Inaruwa, Sunsari, Saptakoshi Consultancy has grown from a small local firm to one of Eastern Nepal's most trusted civil engineering consultancies.

With Registration No. 434, we are a fully licensed and certified engineering consultancy committed to delivering high-quality, precise civil engineering services. Over the past 14+ years, we have successfully completed 500+ projects ranging from residential buildings to infrastructure development.

Our deep understanding of local soil conditions, climate patterns, and municipal regulations makes us the go-to consultancy for clients across Sunsari, Morang, Jhapa, and surrounding districts.
